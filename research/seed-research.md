## Target 1: dnata Logistics, 57,000 m² warehouse, Dubai South

**Verdict:** Commissioning is confirmed by the Emirates Group H1 2026-27 results, released 5 Nov 2026. The sources do not give an exact commissioning date.

**Confirmed (indexed):**
- **Original announcement, 5 Dec 2024.** Published by Dubai Media Office (`mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2024/december/05-12/dnata-logistics-to-expand`) and the dnata media centre. The extract describes "a new 57,000m² warehouse in Dubai South", which "represents an investment of AED 100 million ($27 million)".
  - "construction of the warehouse is underway, with completion scheduled for November 2025"
  - Capacity: 400,000 tonnes a year, storage capacity up 50%, more than 50 jobs.
  - Specs: automated storage and retrieval (ASRS), automated truck loading, AI-driven warehouse management system, solar panels, rainwater and energy harvesting. The facility "expected to achieve the global LEED certification one year after operations begins".
- **Schedule update, April 2025 (IATA World Cargo Symposium release, 14 Apr 2025).** The facility was "due for completion by the end of 2025".
- **Commissioning (Emirates Group H1 2026-27 results, 5 Nov 2026).** The release is on emirates.com, Dubai Media Office (`.../2026/novemebr/05-11/...`), Gulf News, Cargo Newswire and STAT Times. Extract: dnata "commissioned its new AED 100 million (US$ 27 million) logistics facility in Dubai South" in the first half of the 2026-27 financial year.

**Not confirmed:**
- **Exact commissioning or opening date.** No standalone dnata opening release was found.
- **Basis of the 57,000 m².** No source says whether it is building GFA or plot area, so it is recorded as `unspecified`.
- **Grade A wording.** No source uses it.
- **LEED status.** Unknown.

**Conflict:**
- **"Commissioned" or "commissioning".** The emirates.com and Cargo Newswire extracts say "commissioned". The Dubai Media Office extract reads "was commissioning". The status is therefore set to `construction_completed` rather than `operational` until the release is read directly.
- **Completion target moved.** The target shifted from November 2025 to end-2025, and commissioning actually happened in FY H1 2026-27. That is a delay of about 6–10 months, which is inferred and was not explained in any source.

## Main gaps

1. **No primary page was read directly**, because the network policy blocks the news and company hosts. Every fact is "indexed".
2. **Completion has passed its target date but is unconfirmed for four projects:** Toll DC6 (August 2026), Sky Support Complex (Q3 2026), Terralogix Phase 1 (Q3/Q4 2026) and the Cars24 hub (August 2026).
3. **dnata:** there is no exact commissioning date, the area basis is unknown, and the "commissioned" vs "commissioning" wording needs a direct read.
4. **Investment values are missing** for most 2026 openings: DSV, CEVA, Iron Mountain, Hellmann, Aramex and Toll.
5. **Most area figures have an unstated basis.** Only Toll (site vs building), DHL (warehouse vs plot), Aldar 7 Central (GLA) and Al Sharqi (built-up storage vs open yard) are explicit.
