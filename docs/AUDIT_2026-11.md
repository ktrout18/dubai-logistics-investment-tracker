single source of truth; the owner's briefing PDF never overrides a stronger
source. Every one of the 31 records was checked field by field against its

the next step rebuilds the briefing from the audited records and removes them.

  X, TradeArabia, MOL, MarkLines, Splash, Container News, DHL Group, Toll Group.
  Results count as `indexed` only.
* **Dates.** A press-release dateline or a newsroom date is a **publication
  date**. Where a source does not give the event's own day ("has inaugurated",
  "has broken ground", "has also opened"), the event and `actualCompletion`
  are now stored with **month** precision and the record says the day is not
  stated. Day precision is kept only where the source dates the event itself
  (DSV "on 20 May 2026"; Gargash "signed on 13 October"; Emirates "Today's
  groundbreaking"), or where the event *is* the announcement (announcement,
  investment-commitment and status-statement events dated by their release).
  Caveat for the verifier: for openings reported only by a release, the month
  stored is the month of the release; the opening may have been earlier.
* **Area bases.** A size given as "a 57,000m² warehouse" or "spanning 7,200
  square metres" has no stated basis and is `unspecified`. `warehouse_gfa` is
  used only where the source says GFA or built-up storage, `gla` only for
  "leasable", `mixed_building_area` only for built-up area that the source says
  includes offices/HQ/retail. A derived figure is never an area value.
* **Grade A.** `confirmed` only where the developer, owner/occupier or an
  official body states Grade A for this facility in its own release.
